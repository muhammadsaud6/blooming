Questions parents ask

Clear answers to help you decide if Blooming Stars is the right fit, before you call or visit.

The basics

Ages, hours and care options

What ages do you accept?

We care for children from 6 weeks to 12 years old, from infants right through to school-age children.

What are your hours?

We are open Monday to Friday, 8 am to 5 pm. Before and after care are available on request.

Do you offer part-time care?

Yes. We offer full time, part time and extended care, so you can choose the schedule that fits your family.

Is weekend care available?

Yes, weekend care is available. Please get in touch to check which days are open.

Do you care for school-age children?

Yes. School-age children get out-of-school care with homework help and tutoring.

Do you run a summer program?

Yes. Our summer care keeps the fun and routine going when school is out.

Safety and trust

Who will look after my child?

Are you licensed?

Yes. Blooming Stars is a licensed child care home in Maryland, licence no. 259081.

Who runs Blooming Stars?

Mrs. Sadia Ahmed, our Program Director. She started Blooming Stars after more than ten years in Montessori child care.

Is the staff trained in first aid?

Yes. Staff are CPR and first aid certified, and can give medication when needed.

Can you give my child medication?

Yes. Maryland requires a Medication Authorization Form (OCC 1216), completed by your child's doctor, for each medication.

Daily life

What does a day look like?

What will my child do all day?

Every day mixes learning with play: Montessori materials, arts and crafts, sensory play, dramatic play, music and movement, and time outdoors.

What is your teaching approach?

We follow a Montessori and nature-based approach. Hands-on materials build creativity, imagination and independence.

Do you provide snacks?

Yes. Children get a morning snack and an evening snack.

Do the children play outside?

Yes. We have outdoor play space, and children get fresh air every day.

Which languages are spoken?

English, Hindi and Urdu, so your child can hear the languages your family speaks at home.

How big is the group?

We are a small, home-based program, so every child is known by name and gets plenty of attention.

Getting started

Enrollment and fees

How do I enroll my child?

Start by booking a visit and tour. If it feels right, we set up an enrollment meeting to go through the paperwork and plan how your child will settle in.

Which forms do I need?

Maryland asks for an Immunization Certificate, a Health Inventory with a physical exam from the last 12 months, and a blood lead test certificate. We also ask for an "All About Me" form.

All of them are on our Forms page.

How much does care cost?

Fees depend on your child's age and the schedule you need. Call us on (443) 840-0115 or send a message and we will share current rates.

Can I visit before I decide?

Of course. A visit takes about 30 minutes. You meet Mrs. Sadia and see the space. Pick a time here.

Where are you located?

We are in Columbia, Maryland (21045), near Route 108 and Route 175. Call us if you need directions.

Do you have places available?

Places change through the year. The quickest way to check is a phone call booking or a call to (443) 840-0115.

Still have a question?

We are happy to talk it through. Call, send a message or come and see us.
