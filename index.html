Welcome

A warm, safe home for growing minds

Blooming Stars was started by Mrs. Sadia after more than ten years in Montessori child care. Children learn with interactive Montessori materials that build creativity, imagination and independence.

Every day mixes learning with play: arts and crafts, sensory play, dramatic play, music and movement, and time outdoors. It is a nurturing place where curiosity and social skills grow together.

Meals and snacks

We serve a morning and an evening snack every day. Please tell us about any allergies.

Health and medication

Staff are CPR and first aid certified. To give medication we need a Medication Authorization Form (OCC 1216) from your doctor.

Positive guidance

We guide behaviour with patience, praise and clear routines. Never shouting, shaming or physical punishment.

When your child is unwell

Please keep your child home if they are sick, and let us know. We will talk through when they can come back.

Paperwork before day one

Maryland requires immunization, health and lead test forms before your child starts. See the forms.

Staying in touch

Talk to Mrs. Sadia at drop-off and pick-up, or call any time with questions about your child's day.
